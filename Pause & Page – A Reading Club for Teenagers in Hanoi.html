Our solution

Two quiet hours.
Every Saturday.

FAQ

Common questions

"Reading is boring. Why give up my Saturday?"

Reading here is a treat, not homework. There is no grading and no page target. You get snacks, music, games and new friends, and you choose your own book.

"I read slowly, or I don't read much."

You are exactly who we want. Our workshops teach speed reading and note-taking, and our circle talks have no wrong answers.

"Do I really have to turn off my phone?"

Yes, but only for two hours. Airplane mode is enough. Many members say the break feels like a relief.

"Is it free?"

Yes. We ask members to bring one book to share. Snacks are supported by donations.
